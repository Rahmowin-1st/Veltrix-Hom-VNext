# Flashcards — Final

Flashcards persist decks/cards, due queues, AGAIN/HARD/GOOD/EASY review, schedule reset and deck statistics. SRS due state is deterministic; AI may draft content but does not own due dates.

Review activity may feed learning signals and meaningful events. Project/source context is preserved by surrounding flows. Offline/sync foundations persist review-related mutations with stable/idempotent semantics where queued.
