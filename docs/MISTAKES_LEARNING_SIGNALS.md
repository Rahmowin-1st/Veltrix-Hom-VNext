# Mistakes + Learning Signals

Mistakes are durable learning objects and first-class Student Model evidence. Repeated failures are not blindly dumped into memory: mistakes can be resolved, converted to targeted practice/flashcards and summarized into scoped signals.

Personalization maps MISTAKE evidence to targeted practice/review actions. Home/Personal expose active mistake/weakness summaries without giving AI progression/completion authority. Account/Project isolation applies to mistake retrieval and derived signals.
